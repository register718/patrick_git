$k_{B,\mathrm{on}}$ & \num{24.1} & $\mu\mathrm{M}^{-1}\mathrm{s}^{-1}$ & D & Brake activation by PKBR1$^*$; half-activation at 20 PKBR1$^*$ molecules/voxel. \\
