Oligosaccharides and the Enzyme-Inhibitor Interaction
Since both the inhibitor and the enzyme are glycoproteins,
it is possible that theformation of the enzyme-inhibitor complexinvolves a lectin type of binding. The effect of the
following sugars on the formation of the complex was tested:
D-glUCOSe,D-gdaCtOSe, D-mannose, D-fucose, L-fucose, D-ghcosamine,N-aCetyl-D-glUCOSamine,N-acetyl-D-galactosamine,
a-methyl-D-mannoside, a-methyl-D-glucoside, myoinositol,
and a-lactose. A t a final concentration of 0.2 M, none of these
sugars had any effect on either the enzyme activity or the
inhibitor activity.
