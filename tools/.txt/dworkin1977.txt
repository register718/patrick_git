Several previously unavailable
parameters
of adenosine
3’:5’-monophosphate
have been determined.
The molar extinction coefficient
at pH 7.0 is 1.38 x lOma, the aqueous

In attempting to develop an experimental
chemotactic system in which the spatial and temporal dependence of the
concentration of adenosine 3’:5’-monophosphate
(CAMP) were
well characterized,
we found that certain
necessary
data,
namely, the solubility and diffusion coefficient of the CAMP,
were not available. For example, other investigators
concerned with the role of CAMP as a diffusible morphogen (1) and
as a chemotactic agent for polymorphonucleocytes
(2), had
been forced to rely on estimates of these parameters. Since
interest in this compound is likely to continue to grow because
of its ubiquity and importance, we have determined both the
aqueous solubility and diffusion coefficient of CAMP experimentally.
